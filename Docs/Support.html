Cue Support

Cue is a context-aware shopping list for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. If you have questions or run into an issue, reach out — we're happy to help.

Contact

Email us or open a request via the App Store review page. We aim to respond within one business day.

Common Help Topics

  • How does automatic list switching work? — Cue monitors geofences you set on each Context. When your device enters the region, that Context becomes active and Cue surfaces it on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Why does my list not switch automatically? — Make sure you've granted "Always On" location permission in Settings → Cue, and that you've set a location trigger on your Context. iOS limits apps to 20 monitored regions at a time; Cue rebalances them as you move.
  • How do I add items from my Apple Watch? — Open Cue on your Watch, tap the mic icon, and dictate your item using Voice Capture. Microphone and speech recognition permissions are required.
  • Can I use Cue without an iCloud account? — Yes. Cue works fully offline and on a single device. iCloud sync is optional and activates automatically when you're signed in with an Apple ID that has iCloud Drive enabled.
